About Snackify

Snackify brings snacks and treats together in one easy-to-browse menu. Explore the popular picks above, then open the menu to see the available items and prices.

When you're ready, choose your snacks and quantities and complete the order form on the menu page. Your order is submitted to Snackify's existing external ordering service; check the confirmation or error message there for the result.
